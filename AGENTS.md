Basic Rules for Agents and other AI contributors.
- Adhere to the Coding Style and Standards.
- Uphold code quality.
- Make frequent small (but well scoped and maninfull) commits with, expressive comments.
- Be transparent about your use: add yourself as contributor on commits you make.
- Maintain the Documentation
	- document what you are doing
	- when editing docs ensure the rest is also up to date and you don't break anything
	- even whan you just read the docs, note and fix any inaccuracies or deprecated info.

In addition to the basic rule here follow the Projects Specific Rules.
